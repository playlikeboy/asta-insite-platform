## D-05 in detail

Apple guideline 3.1.1 says: "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app... you must use in-app purchase." Guideline 3.1.3(e) lets physical goods and services "consumed outside of the app" use other payment methods. A provider subscription that unlocks digital leads inside the app may fall under 3.1.1, even though the provider's eventual work is physical.

Google Play's payments policy similarly requires Play Billing for "access to in-app features or services", and exempts "physical services". It also mentions programs for alternative billing in "eligible countries/regions".

Options:

| Option                                           | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Store billing on mobile, Razorpay on web      | Most likely to pass review. Store fees apply. Two billing systems to reconcile.                                                                                                        |
| B. Razorpay inside the app                       | Simplest and matches the proposal. Real rejection risk.                                                                                                                                |
| C. Provider pays on a website; app sells nothing | Avoids in-app purchase flow. Guideline 3.1.3(b) permits access to web-bought subscriptions only if they are "also available as in-app purchases", so this alone may not satisfy Apple. |
| D. Google alternative billing                    | Depends on India eligibility, which was not verified.                                                                                                                                  |

This is not legal advice. Get a written answer from App Review or a lawyer before M4. The `billing_provider` column keeps all options open. See [Subscription model](../product/SUBSCRIPTION_MODEL.md).

## Technical decision that differs from the blueprint

The blueprint proposed storing chat in Appwrite TablesDB. Research led to storing messages in PostgreSQL and using TablesDB only as a realtime signal. See [ADR-004](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#adr-004-messages-live-in-postgresql-with-tablesdb-as-a-realtime-signal). The developer can overturn it if spike S-03 fails.
